Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Publisher: NOVAA Studios

Contact: nova.team.support@gmail.com

1. What We Collect

Fixora is local-first: your code and your keys stay on your machine. Two things do leave it, and only when you choose — signing in, and submitting feedback. Both are listed below.

Data Collected? Where it goes
Your source code Never Stays on your machine (except to your AI provider)
API keys Never Stored encrypted locally only
Your name/email Only if you sign in Sign-in is optional. If you use Google or GitHub sign-in, your provider gives us your name, email and avatar, held by Supabase. Skip sign-in and we never see them.
Feedback you submit Only if you send it Star rating, your optional comment, and the app version — see §3
Your Gumroad license key If you buy GO or PRO Stored on your device only — see §4
Usage analytics / telemetry Never The app contains no analytics of any kind. This website uses Google Analytics, and only after you accept the cookie banner.

2. Your AI Provider

When you run a repair, your code is sent to the AI provider you configured. That provider’s own privacy policy applies. We recommend reviewing:

  • Google Gemini Privacy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI Privacy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic Privacy: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Groq Privacy: groq.com/privacy-policy

3. Feedback

Fixora may ask how it is working for you after a few repairs. Answering is optional, and declining twice stops it asking again.

When you submit feedback we store:

  • Your star rating (1–5)
  • Your comment, if you write one
  • The app version, so we know which build the feedback is about
  • Your account id, if you are signed in — anonymous otherwise

This is stored in Supabase (ap-south-1 — Mumbai, India), a cloud database provider. Feedback is not shared with anyone else and is not used for advertising.

Publishing is opt-in. Your feedback stays private unless you tick “Show this on the Fixora website”. If you do, your rating and comment may appear on our public feedback page. Your name, email and account id are never published — only the rating, the comment and the month.

Withdrawing consent. Email nova.team.support@gmail.com to request removal of your feedback, and we will delete it — from the public page and from the database. Tell us roughly when you sent it, or the email you signed in with, so we can find it.

4. Your License Key

If you buy GO or PRO, Gumroad gives you a license key. Fixora stores that key on your device only, in the app’s own data folder, and uses it to re-check your plan with Gumroad about once a day so a refunded or cancelled purchase stops unlocking paid features.

The key is sent to Gumroad and nobody else, and only to verify it. NOVAA Studios does not receive or store it.

5. Local Storage

Fixora stores the following on your machine only:

  • Your encrypted API keys
  • Your provider preferences and settings
  • Repair history (local database)
  • Your license key and repair count (see §4)

None of this is transmitted to NOVAA Studios.

6. Telemetry

The Fixora app contains no telemetry or analytics of any kind. It does not count launches, features used, or errors, and there is nothing to opt into or out of.

This website uses Google Analytics, and only after you accept the cookie banner. Decline and no analytics script is loaded at all.

7. Children’s Privacy

Fixora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you via the app or website for significant changes.
