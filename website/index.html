The pipeline is the product

Every repair is verified before you trust it

"The AI suggested this" is worth little. "This fix resolves the finding, compiles, and breaks no other check" is worth shipping.

01 · GROUND

Deterministic analysis

Your own tree-sitter, ESLint, tsc, ruff, mypy, go vet — findings with a rule, a location, and evidence. Zero AI here.

02 · REPAIR

Grounded, gated AI

The model fixes one grounded finding. A secret gate blocks any key or credential from ever reaching the provider.

03 · VERIFY

Re-run on a safe copy

The fix is applied to a throwaway overlay and your analyzers re-run. Verified or Regression — honestly.

04 · APPLY

You decide

Review the diff, apply with one click, and keep a local audit trail of every repair. Regressions can't be applied.

Trust, as testable claims

Your code stays yours

These aren't marketing lines — they're how the product is built.

  • Bring your own key. AI runs straight from the app to the provider you choose. There is no Fixora server on the AI path.
  • Your key is encrypted with your OS keychain and never leaves the main process — the UI never even sees it.
  • A secret gate scans every outbound payload and refuses to send a file containing an API key, private key, or token — naming the file and the rule.
  • Nothing is retained. No code, paths, prompts, or findings are logged or stored off your machine. The repair history is local SQLite, on your disk.
  • Local-first. Analysis and verification run on your machine. The cloud can't search what it never receives.
